\section*{Research Profile}
Cosmologist specializing in galaxy surveys, growth of large-scale structure, and statistical methods for cosmological inference. Research experience spans field-level inference, effective-field-theory likelihoods, multi-tracer analyses, beyond-two-point statistics, and machine-learning methods for survey data. Current and former survey affiliations include PFS and DESI.

\section*{Research Projects and Expertise}
\begin{tightitemize}
  \item \textbf{Galaxy clustering at the field level:} first-author PRL study on how much information can be extracted from galaxy clustering at the field level.
  \item \textbf{EFT likelihoods and physical data models:} work on consistency tests of field-level inference with the EFT likelihood and on the impact of physical data models in forward inference.
  \item \textbf{Multi-survey calibration:} current first-author work, ``Multi-tracers, multi-surveys: a joint Fisher analysis of DESI+PFS.''
  \item \textbf{Growth of structure and dark-sector tests:} first-author PRL evidence for suppressed structure growth and co-authored work on Horndeski growth-rate parameterization.
  \item \textbf{Beyond-two-point survey statistics:} contribution to a parameter-masked mock data challenge for beyond-two-point galaxy clustering statistics.
  \item \textbf{Machine learning for reconstruction:} project on reconstructing the local density field with combined convolutional and point-cloud architecture.
  \item \textbf{CMB--large-scale-structure connections:} first-author kSZ forward-modeling work including uncertainties from velocity reconstruction.
\end{tightitemize}
