## Tone requirements
The tone should be:

- professional;
- direct;
- scientifically serious;
- mission-oriented;
- technically credible;
- not desperate;
- not overly self-promotional;
- not generic.

The user wants to convey intense commitment to science and to this opportunity, but without sounding desperate. Do not say anything like:

- "I just need one chance."
- "I have never had an interview."
- "Before I leave academia."
- "I would be grateful for any opportunity."
- "I know I can prove myself."
- "I am desperate to continue."

Instead, translate that into professional language such as:

> This position is unusually well aligned with the research program I have been building, and I would approach it with focus, care, and mission-oriented commitment.

A strong closing idea:

> I would approach this role with the focus and commitment of someone who sees Roman and Euclid as the natural place for field-level and forward-modeling methods to become useful at mission scale.

- His scientific mission is extracting otherwise inaccessible information from cosmic structure to test growth, gravity, dark energy, and primordial physics.
