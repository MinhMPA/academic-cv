Title: Field-level and forward modeling for Roman and Euclid dark-sector cosmology

Target length: no more than 3 pages, following the active job posting.

## 1. Research vision

- Roman/Euclid will test dark energy and gravity through weak lensing and galaxy clustering.
- The limiting challenge is robust modeling: nonlinear structure, galaxy bias, intrinsic alignments, selection, covariance, and likelihood validation.
- Minh's thesis: field-level and forward-modeling methods can help determine what information is robust and mission-usable.

## 2. Preparation: methods and science background

- Multi-tracer and multi-survey calibration work with DESI+PFS.
- Intrinsic alignment paper as weak-lensing-systematics bridge.

## 3. Proposed JPL contribution

- Contribute to joint weak-lensing and clustering modeling.
- Develop and validate simulation-based likelihood tests.
- Build diagnostics linking summary statistics, higher-order statistics, and field-level information.
- Translate results into dark-sector interpretation.

## 4. Three-year integration and independent niche

- Year 1: integrate into Roman/Euclid science teams and current modeling/likelihood work.
- Years 1-2: lead focused validation project in overlap/cross-probe analysis.
- Years 2-3: deliver paper/tool, connect field-level validation to mission-ready summaries, grow independent niche.
