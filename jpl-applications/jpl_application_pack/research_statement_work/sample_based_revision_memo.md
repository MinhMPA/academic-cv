## What The Samples Add

| Panel weakness | Useful sample evidence | How to use it | Risk control |
|---|---|---|---|
| The current opening is scientifically correct but generic. | CU Boulder opens with the central question: optimal data representation for the physics being tested. | Add a sharper thesis around likelihood validation and information accounting. | Avoid making the statement sound like a general field-level manifesto. |
| Scientist IV leadership sounds broad. | KAKENHI uses milestones and named products: simulation suite, systematics variations, validation, application. | Define a flagship Roman--Euclid overlap mock challenge or likelihood-validation work package. | Do not claim existing Roman/Euclid leadership. |

## Sample Language To Avoid

- "only FLI"
- "breakthrough"
- "unwavering conviction"
- "future of cosmological analysis"
- "I am uniquely positioned" unless immediately grounded in verifiable local evidence

## Recommended Revision Theme

The revised statements should present Minh as a scientist whose distinctive value is not simply field-level inference, but disciplined validation of how Roman and Euclid data are compressed, modeled, calibrated, and interpreted for dark-sector physics.
